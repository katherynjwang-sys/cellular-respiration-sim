01 / THE INPUTS & OUTPUTS
◉

Food → Glucose

Carbohydrates in food can be broken down into glucose, a fuel molecule rich in chemical energy.

◎

Inhaled O₂

Oxygen reaches your cells through your blood. It accepts electrons at the end of the electron transport chain.

⊙

Exhaled CO₂

Carbon from glucose is released as carbon dioxide, carried to your lungs by blood, and breathed out.

Glucose + Oxygen → Carbon dioxide + Water + ATP
C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂ → 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O + usable energy

03 / CONNECT THE DOTS

One process. Three big connections.

01 — The carbon trail

The carbon atoms in glucose are gradually released as CO₂ during pyruvate oxidation and the Krebs cycle. Blood carries that CO₂ to the lungs.

02 — The oxygen role

O₂ accepts electrons at the end of the electron transport chain. This allows electron flow to continue and supports the proton gradient that powers ATP synthase.

03 — The energy payoff

Energy from food is transferred into ATP, the cell's usable energy currency. Most ATP from aerobic respiration is produced during stage 4.
